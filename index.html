Loading the emulator…

What this is. A JavaScript port of METEOR's generation kernel, driven by a compact bundle exported from a model trained offline on CMIP6 output. The whole payload is about 300 KB and there is no server: the emulator runs on your machine.

Two generations of scenario. The CMIP6 SSPs and the CMIP7 ScenarioMIP markers were built years apart and harmonized to different vintages of history, so they are not interchangeable. Both are driven here through the same simple climate model, CICERO-SCM, which is what makes comparing them meaningful at all: taking CMIP7's forcing from its own MAGICC assessment instead would have put a 0.74 W/m² model difference inside what looks like a scenario difference.

What it is not. One model, NorESM2-MM, so the spread you see is internal variability — not model uncertainty, and not the full uncertainty in a projection. Maps show the forced response only; variability at a region you draw yourself would need a further 11 MB of EOF maps that this page does not load. Custom emissions need a simple climate model, which is not here either, so the scenarios on offer are the ones the bundle was built with. Precipitation covers 2015–2100 only, the window its distribution transform was fitted for.
