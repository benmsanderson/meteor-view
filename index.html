METEOR explorer

A climate emulator running entirely in your browser. Pick a scenario or draw your own warming pathway, pick a place, and see the spread of monthly outcomes.

Global warming pathway

Drag across the box to draw global mean warming through 2100. The emulator rescales the forced response to follow it, so a place warms more or less than the global mean in the way the model says it should.

Loading the emulator…

What this is. A JavaScript port of METEOR's generation kernel, driven by a compact bundle exported from a model trained offline on CMIP6 output. The whole payload is about 300 KB and there is no server: the emulator runs on your machine.

What it is not. The bundle carries no gridded output, so there are no maps here. Custom emissions scenarios need a simple climate model, which is also not in the bundle — the drawn pathway rescales a bundled scenario rather than running new emissions. Precipitation is shown for 2015–2100 only, the window its distribution transform was fitted for.
