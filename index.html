Loading the emulator…

What this is. A JavaScript port of METEOR's generation kernel, driven by a compact bundle exported from a model trained offline on CMIP6 output. The whole payload is about 300 KB and there is no server: the emulator runs on your machine.

What it is not. One model, NorESM2-MM, so the spread you see is internal variability — not model uncertainty, and not the full uncertainty in a projection. Maps show the forced response only; variability at a region you draw yourself would need a further 11 MB of EOF maps that this page does not load. Custom emissions need a simple climate model, which is not here either — a drawn pathway rescales a bundled scenario rather than running new emissions. Precipitation covers 2015–2100 only, the window its distribution transform was fitted for.
